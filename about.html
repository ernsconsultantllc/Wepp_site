About Erns Consultant

Helping students prepare for healthcare opportunities in sterile processing and central service.

Erns Consultant provides healthcare training for students interested in sterile processing and central service technician careers.

Our goal is to help students understand important healthcare safety practices including infection control, sterilization, medical instruments, packaging, storage, and handling.
